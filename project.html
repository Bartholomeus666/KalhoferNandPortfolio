Rebuilding the checkout flow

  • RoleProduct designer
  • Year2025
  • ClientNorthline Retail

The problem

Northline's checkout had a 61% abandonment rate. Customers dropped off mostly at the shipping and payment steps, where the form asked for too much information too early. Replace the "problem" section with what actually happened on your project.

project image or screenshot

What I did

I audited the existing flow, mapped every field back to whether it was actually required at that step, and cut the checkout from five pages to two. Guest checkout became the default path instead of an opt-out.

project image or screenshot

Outcome

Abandonment dropped from 61% to 38% within the first month after launch, and support tickets about checkout errors fell by half.
